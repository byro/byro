# User guide

This section is for everyone who works with an already running byro
day to day - board members, treasurers, staff - not for administrators who
install or operate byro (see [Installation](../installation/index.md),
[Administration](../administration/operations.md) and
[Configuration](../administration/index.md) for that).

- [Members](members.md): search, create, edit, joining and leaving,
  documents, export/import, bulk actions.
- [Memberships and fees](memberships-and-fees.md): how the fee, payment
  interval and statute of limitations fit together.
- [Member page](member-page.md): the access members get themselves, without a
  login - their data, the member list, change proposals and privacy settings.
- [Finances](finances.md): accounts, transactions and bank import.
- [Documents](documents.md): categories and where documents are uploaded.
- [Communication](mails.md): mail templates, composing, the outbox and PGP
  from a user's perspective.
- [My account](account.md): your own profile, your personal API token -
  reachable from your user menu, not from Administration.
- [Multi-factor authentication](mfa.md): setting up MFA for your own
  account, signing in, recovery codes.
